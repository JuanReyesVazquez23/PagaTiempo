# JTBD — Registro de cuotas

## Job statement

When a student brings cash (or reports a transfer), I want to find them quickly and add the amount paid, so I can see which of the 10 months are covered without mixing students.

## Persona

- **Who**: Tesorera of the group
- **Skill**: Comfortable with phone/laptop forms, not a developer
- **Device**: Laptop in a meeting, sometimes a phone
- **Frequency**: Several times per week during the 10-month cycle
- **Failure cost**: Wrong student or lost history → conflict and missing funds

## Current pain (incumbent)

Spreadsheets or paper lists: easy to overwrite, hard to search, no per-student timeline.

## Success

Student found in seconds, payment stored, month grid and history match the cash received.
